# Product labels, buffer zones and records

Source: Directive 2009/128/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council on the sustainable use of pesticides; Defra/HSE (2006), Code of Practice for Using Plant Protection Products

## The label comes first

The product label is the legal and practical reference for application rates, crops, timing, weather limits, buffer zones and protective equipment. General guidance never overrides the label.

## Buffer zones and sensitive areas

Labels and national rules may require untreated buffer zones next to surface water, field margins and other sensitive areas. Measures to protect the aquatic environment and drinking water are a core part of the EU framework for sustainable pesticide use.

## Protective equipment and records

Wear the personal protective equipment required by the label when mixing and spraying. Keep records of what was applied, where, when, at what rate and in which weather, as required by national rules.
