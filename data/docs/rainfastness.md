# Rain after application and rainfastness

Source: Defra/HSE (2006), Code of Practice for Using Plant Protection Products; product labels

## Rainfastness

The rainfast period is the time a product needs after application before rain no longer reduces its effect. It differs between products and formulations, from less than an hour to many hours, so the label is the reference.

## Planning around rain

Check the forecast for the hours after spraying, not only the current weather. If rain is likely within the rainfast period, postpone the application. Heavy rain soon after spraying also increases the risk of run-off into ditches and watercourses.

## Wet foliage

Spraying onto leaves that are already wet from rain or heavy dew can dilute the spray and make it run off the leaf. Wait until the crop has dried unless the label says otherwise.
