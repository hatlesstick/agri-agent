# Temperature, humidity and Delta T

Source: GRDC (Grains Research and Development Corporation), spray application guidance on Delta T

## Evaporation of droplets

In hot and dry air droplets evaporate quickly. They become smaller, drift further and may not reach the target, while very humid and cool conditions slow drying on the leaf.

## Delta T

Delta T is the difference between the dry-bulb and wet-bulb air temperature and is a simple indicator of evaporation conditions. Values of about 2 to 8 °C are commonly described as favourable for spraying, above about 10 °C as unfavourable, and very low values can point to inversion-prone, humid conditions.

## Practical use

When the day is getting hot and dry, spray earlier in the morning once any inversion has lifted, or later when temperature falls, and choose coarser droplets where the label allows.
