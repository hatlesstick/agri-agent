# Wind speed for spraying with field-crop sprayers

Source: Code of Practice for Using Plant Protection Products (Defra and HSE), Section 4, Table 6 "A guide to wind speed and using field-crop sprayers with conventional nozzles"
Updated: 2026-11-26

## Wind speed guide for field-crop sprayers

The Code gives a guide that relates the Beaufort scale, measured 10 metres above the ground, to the wind speed at the height of the spray nozzles, for standard field-crop sprayers with conventional nozzles. The relation assumes a crop covers the ground. Without crop or grass cover the wind at nozzle height is higher.

| Beaufort force | Description | Visible signs | Guide for a standard crop sprayer | Wind speed at nozzle height |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | Calm | Smoke rises vertically | Use only medium or coarse spray quality | Less than 2 kilometres an hour (less than 1.2 miles an hour) |
| 1 | Light air | Smoke drifts, showing the wind direction | Acceptable spraying conditions | 2 to 3.2 kilometres an hour (1.2 to 2 miles an hour) |
| 2 | Light breeze | Leaves rustle and you can feel the wind on your face | Ideal spraying conditions | 3.2 to 6.5 kilometres an hour (2 to 4 miles an hour) |
| 3 | Gentle breeze | Leaves and twigs are constantly moving | Increased risk of spray drift; avoid spraying herbicides and take special care with other pesticides | 6.5 to 9.6 kilometres an hour (4 to 6 miles an hour) |
| 4 | Moderate breeze | Small branches are moved and dust and loose paper are raised | Do not spray | 9.6 to 14.5 kilometres an hour (6 to 9 miles an hour) |

## Converted to metres per second

The table above is the Code's own figure in kilometres per hour. The metres-per-second values below are a conversion (1 m/s = 3.6 km/h), not figures from the Code.

- Calm: below 0.56 m/s at nozzle height.
- Acceptable: 0.56 to 0.89 m/s.
- Ideal: 0.89 to 1.8 m/s.
- Increased drift risk: 1.8 to 2.7 m/s.
- Do not spray: from 2.7 m/s at nozzle height (about 4.0 m/s at the top of force 4).

## Related guidance in the same Code section

- Do not apply a pesticide if it is likely to drift off target or if wind may carry spray droplets or vapour away from the target area.
- Check the weather forecast and the conditions at the site before you start.
- Low winds on warm sunny afternoons with low humidity, and temperatures above 30 °C, are listed as conditions to avoid.
- The Code does not give a numeric limit for wind gusts. Gust limits are not covered by this document.
